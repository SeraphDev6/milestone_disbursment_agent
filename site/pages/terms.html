2. What the service does

Milestone Ledger turns a grant or statement of work into tranches with acceptance criteria, lets each party confirm those criteria, holds the payer's funds at PayPal, takes in evidence, checks it against the criteria with deterministic checks and an AI verifier, routes close calls to people, and settles each tranche through PayPal. It keeps an audit log and produces compliance packets.

The service has two modes. Sandbox is connected to PayPal's sandbox: no real money moves. Live is connected to PayPal's production API, pays connected payees directly, and requires PayPal partner approval. The hosted service tells you on every page which mode it is in.

5. Payments and PayPal

  • All payments are made through PayPal under PayPal's user agreement and its fees. You need a PayPal account to pay or be paid, and PayPal may decline, hold or reverse a payment under its own rules.
  • Funds held for a tranche are held by PayPal under an authorization the payer approved, not by us. In live mode payment goes from the payer to the payee directly; we never hold funds.
  • PayPal authorizations expire. If a hold expires before a tranche verifies, the payer may need to fund it again.
  • After a capture there is a contest window during which the payer may contest the tranche. Contests and PayPal disputes pause payment to the payee until a reviewer resolves them or, failing that, until PayPal's own timelines apply. The ledger shows the window for each tranche.
  • We are not responsible for PayPal's acts or omissions, for exchange rates, or for a payee's failure to connect or maintain a PayPal account that can receive payment.

6. Verification and automatic release

  • Verdicts combine deterministic checks with an AI verifier that returns a confidence and citations. A verdict is an assessment, not a guarantee that the work meets the criteria. The verifier can be wrong, and evidence can be misleading.
  • Each organization chooses whether tranches can release automatically and the confidence it requires. If you turn on automatic release, you accept that a tranche meeting your threshold will be captured and released without a person looking at it first, subject to the contest window.
  • Below the threshold, or with automatic release off, a person with the right role decides. The record of who decided, and on what, is in the audit log.
  • You must not try to influence a verdict other than by submitting genuine evidence. Instructions hidden in evidence, fabricated artefacts and tampered check results are a breach of these terms and may be an offence.

7. Deliverables we hold

  • When an agreement withholds work until payment, we hold the payee's deliverables, encrypted, and release them to the payer when the tranche is captured. Until then the payer sees only what the agreement allows, such as a commit's tree and its check results.
  • The payee keeps all rights in the work until the agreement between the parties says otherwise. We take no rights in it beyond what we need to hold, verify and hand it over.
  • Retention of originals after a tranche closes follows the deployment's retention setting, as described in the Privacy Policy. Hashes are kept so the record stays verifiable.
  • If a handover fails its integrity check, it fails closed and both sides are told. We are not responsible for the content of deliverables.
